My Plan

I chose the student performance scenario. My program asks the user to enter subject names, scores out of 100, and credit hours for each subject. I store the data in three parallel arrays — String[] for subjects, int[] for scores, and int[] for credits.

For validation, the subject name cannot be empty. Scores must be between 0 and 100, and if the user types something wrong the program asks again. Credit hours must be between 1 and 6, same thing if it is wrong.

For classification I use a switch statement to give letter grades like A+, A, B, C, and F. I also have a method called classify that is overloaded — one version takes a single score and one version takes the average of all scores to say if the student is Distinction, Pass, or At Risk.

The program calculates the total, simple average, weighted average, highest and lowest score with the subject name, and how many subjects passed the threshold.

Modification Explanation

I added a second field called credit hours for each subject using another parallel array int[] credits. Before I only had subject name and score, and the average was just total divided by how many subjects. Now readData also asks for credit hours between 1 and 6. I made a new method weightedAverage that does sum of score times credit divided by sum of credits. The report prints the credit for each row and shows both the simple average and weighted average so you can see the difference when one subject has more credits than others.
